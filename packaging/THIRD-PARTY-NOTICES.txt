SketchBillet: third-party notices
=================================

SketchBillet is free and open source, under the Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE.txt).
Its source code is at https://github.com/ddreeves70/sketchbillet

SketchBillet is built on the open-source work below. Each one keeps its own
license. The LGPL libraries are separate shared libraries (.dll on Windows,
.dylib on macOS, .so on Linux) that you may replace with your own build of the
same version. Their full license texts are in the licenses folder next to
this file.

Want the exact source code of any library below, as built into this copy of
SketchBillet? Every version is pinned in SketchBillet's repository (vcpkg.json,
cmake/SbAds.cmake, third_party/), or ask in
https://github.com/ddreeves70/sketchbillet/discussions and we'll send it to you.
